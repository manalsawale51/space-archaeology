Why did Opportunity stop communicating?

✓ Case concluded

Opportunity was designed for 90 sols but operated for more than 14 years. A major dust storm in 2018 reduced sunlight reaching its solar panels. After its final communication on June 10, 2018 and months of recovery attempts, NASA declared the mission complete on February 13, 2019.
