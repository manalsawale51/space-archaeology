PHASE 01 / CHRONOLOGY

Mission timeline

Select an event to open its archive note.

JAN 2004
Opportunity lands in Meridiani Planum, beginning a mission planned to last 90 sols.
2004–2017
The rover studies Martian rocks and soil and greatly exceeds its planned mission duration.
JUN 2018
A major dust storm darkens the sky and reduces sunlight reaching the rover’s solar panels.
JUN 10, 2018
Opportunity sends its final communication to Earth.
FEB 2019
NASA concludes the mission after attempts to re-establish contact.
