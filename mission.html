CASE FILE / MER-B

Meet Opportunity

A Mars rover designed for 90 Martian days became a mission lasting more than 14 years.

OBJECTIVE

Search for signs of ancient water

Opportunity investigated rocks and soil to understand whether conditions on Mars could once have supported liquid water.

LANDING

January 2004

Opportunity landed in Meridiani Planum, a region selected for its geological evidence related to past water.

DURATION

90 sols planned

The rover greatly exceeded its original planned mission duration.

MISSION END

February 2019

After a major dust storm interrupted communications in 2018 and recovery attempts failed, NASA concluded the mission.
